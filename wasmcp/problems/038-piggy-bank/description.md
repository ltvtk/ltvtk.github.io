## 🎮 Level 1-038 · Piggy Bank

```
  🐷 SMASH!
  ──────────────────────────
  X bills × 1,000  = X,000
  Y bills × 2,000  = Y,000
  Z bills × 5,000  = Z,000
  ──────────────────────────
  Total = X×1000 + Y×2000 + Z×5000
```

Little Miu has been saving up in her cute piggy bank for months.
She counts **X** bills of 1,000, **Y** bills of 2,000, and **Z** bills of 5,000 inside.
How much money did she save in total?

---

### 📥 Input

```
X    ← count of 1,000-bills
Y    ← count of 2,000-bills
Z    ← count of 5,000-bills
```

### 📤 Output

```
[X×1000 + Y×2000 + Z×5000]
```

---

### 🧪 Examples

| Input | Output |
|---|---|
| `2`↵`1`↵`1` | `9000` |
| `0`↵`0`↵`1` | `5000` |

**Constraint:** $0 \leq X, Y, Z \leq 100$
