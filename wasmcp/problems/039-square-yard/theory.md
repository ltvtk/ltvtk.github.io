## Theory: Area of a Square

The area of a square is side × side = A².

```python
a = int(input())
print(a * a)
```

**Key facts:**
- Area of a square = side × side = A². You can also write `a ** 2` in Python for "a squared".
- `a * a` and `a ** 2` give the same result.
- When A = 1000, the area = 1,000,000. Python handles big numbers without any problem.
- This formula works because a square has 4 equal sides and its area fills a perfect grid of A×A unit squares.
- Geometry problems often start with simple area formulas like this.
