Need help with PyGround or PyGround Pro? Email us at CONTACT_EMAIL. Tell us your device, iOS or macOS version, and what happened.

Common questions

  • Where are my projects? On your device, in the app's home screen. Use the export button to save a backup (.ltvz file) and the import button to restore it.
  • How do I move projects between iPad, iPhone and Mac? Export the workspace on one device, then import the .ltvz file on the other.
  • How do I remove ads? Tap Remove Ads in the bottom bar and subscribe. On a new device, tap Restore Purchases.
  • How do I cancel the subscription? Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions › PyGround.
  • Games use arrow keys. Arrow-key controls need a hardware keyboard.
  • Lessons: youtube.com/@pythonground
